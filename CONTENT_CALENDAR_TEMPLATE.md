## 💡 Content Creation Hacks

### Repurpose Everything

**One YouTube video becomes:**
- 5-10 short clips (Shorts/TikTok/Reels)
- Blog post (transcript + editing)
- Email newsletter
- LinkedIn article
- Twitter thread (key points)
- Instagram carousel (quotes)
- Podcast episode
- Lead magnet (expanded)

### Use Templates

**LinkedIn Post Template:**
```
[Hook - bold statement]

Here's why:

[Point 1]
[Point 2]
[Point 3]

The result? [Outcome]

Try this: [Action]

[Engagement question]
```

**Twitter Thread Template:**
```
1/ [Hook + promise]

2/ [Point]
3/ [Point]
4/ [Point]
5/ [Point]

6/ [Summary + CTA]
```

### Batch Create

**One 4-hour session per month:**
- Record 4 videos
- Write 4 blog outlines
- Create 30 social posts
- Schedule everything

**Time saved:** 20+ hours/month

## 📊 Platform-Specific Tips

### LinkedIn
- **Best times:** Tue-Thu, 7-9am, 12-1pm, 5-6pm
- **Ideal length:** 150-300 words
- **Use:** Line breaks for readability
- **Engage:** Comment on others' posts daily
- **Hashtags:** 3-5 relevant tags

### Twitter
- **Best times:** Mon-Fri, 8am, 12pm, 5pm
- **Thread strategy:** 5-7 tweets ideal
- **Engage:** Reply to bigger accounts
- **Retweet:** Others' content (build relationships)

### YouTube
- **Upload:** Consistently (same day/time weekly)
- **Titles:** Include keywords + numbers
- **Thumbnails:** Text + face + contrast
- **CTAs:** Ask for subscribe/like/comment

### Email
- **Send:** Tuesday/Thursday mornings
- **Subject lines:** Curiosity + benefit
- **Length:** 200-400 words for newsletters
- **CTA:** One clear action per email

## 🎯 Remember

**Consistency > Perfection**

Post daily. Even if it's not perfect. Algorithms reward consistency.

**Value First, Sales Second**

90% value, 10% promotion. Build trust before asking for the sale.

**Engage, Don't Broadcast**

Social media is a conversation. Reply to comments. Ask questions. Build relationships.

**Measure and Adapt**

Track what works. Do more of it. Cut what doesn't.
