## 🚨 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

### ❌ Don't:
1. **Perfectionism** - Ship imperfect content. Iteration beats perfection.
2. **Inconsistency** - Post daily. Even if small. Algorithms reward consistency.
3. **Selling too hard** - Provide value first. Sales follow trust.
4. **Ignoring metrics** - What gets measured gets improved.
5. **Working IN vs ON** - Automate and systematize. Don't be the bottleneck.
6. **Underpricing** - You're worth more than you think. Premium pricing = premium clients.
7. **Trying everything** - Master one platform before adding another.

### ✅ Do:
1. **Focus on ONE platform** initially (LinkedIn if B2B/professional)
2. **Create DAILY** - Consistency compounds
3. **Engage BEFORE selling** - Build relationships
4. **Batch content** - 4 hours once/month > daily scramble
5. **Follow up** - 80% of sales happen in follow-up
6. **Collect testimonials** - Social proof = credibility
7. **Test and iterate** - Data > assumptions
